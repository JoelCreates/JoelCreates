Big-screen play

Put it on the TV — optional.

The game works on your current device. A TV simply makes it bigger for the room.

💻 Laptop → TVMost reliable: connect an HDMI cable, choose the TV as the display, then tap Full Screen.
📱 iPhone / iPadUse Screen Mirroring / AirPlay with a compatible TV or Apple TV.
🤖 AndroidUse Cast, Smart View or your phone maker’s screen-mirroring feature.
🌐 Zoom / TeamsOpen the game on the host device and share that screen with remote guests.
